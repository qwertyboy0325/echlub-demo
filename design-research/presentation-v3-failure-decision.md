# Presentation V3 Failure Decision

**Status:** Owner-confirmed
**Decision date:** 2026-07-28
**Evaluated branch:** `presentation/v3-choreography`
**Evaluated recording:** `artifacts/presentation-v3-choreography/presentation-v3-choreography.mp4`

## Decision

Presentation V3 is a failed product-narrative experiment.

The implementation and its evidence remain useful engineering artifacts, but
the recording must not be presented as proof that EchLub has demonstrated a
compelling collaborative-composition product or differentiator.

## Observed failure

The persistent presenter cursors flash, overlap, and compete for attention.
They signal that several scripted actors exist, but they do not make
collaboration understandable.

The demo implements fork, edit, review, ready, stage, launch, Shared Master,
Mixer/Dock, and restart transitions. Those state transitions show workflow
coverage. They do not show the missing product consequence:

> one person's musical decision changes another person's work, and their
> responses create a result that neither would have produced alone.

Instead, the recording reads primarily as sequential automation:

> actor A changes UI state, then actor B performs the next scripted action.

Captions, lifecycle labels, and cursor choreography carry too much of the
explanation. The musical and visual consequences do not independently establish
a feeling of collaboration for a cold viewer.

## Product consequence

Collaborative composition is not an accepted current EchLub selling point.

This decision does not claim that musical collaboration can never have product
value. It states that:

- generic collaborative composition is not a current differentiator;
- Presentation V3 did not establish a legible collaborative musical payoff;
- cursor, presence, and scripted multi-actor activity are not substitutes for
  evidence of user value;
- any narrower musical proposal/review hypothesis remains research-only until
  it beats an explicit existing-workflow baseline.

## Repository and publication consequence

- Preserve the branch, implementation, recording, and evidence.
- Do not describe Presentation V3 as a successful product demo.
- Do not promote the current recording from the GitHub profile or portfolio.
- Do not continue cursor or choreography polish as if it can repair the missing
  product thesis.
- Do not infer a replacement product headline from this failure.

Future work requires a separately approved, falsifiable product hypothesis.
